CipherON privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026

CipherON is a password manager for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: CipherON does not collect your data. Everything stays on your phone, encrypted, unless you yourself choose to back it up to your own Google Drive or your own WebDAV server.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The developer operates no servers for CipherON, has no user accounts, and receives no passwords, vault contents, usage statistics, crash reports, identifiers or location data. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking libraries.

Data stored on your phone

  • Your vault (entries, notes, attachments, history) is stored in a database encrypted with AES-256 (SQLCipher). The key is derived from your master password with Argon2id. The master password itself is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • If you enable fingerprint unlock, a key protected by the Android Keystore unlocks the vault after your fingerprint is accepted. CipherON never receives your fingerprint data; Android handles it.
  • App settings and sign-in tokens for optional services are stored in the app's private storage; secrets among them are encrypted with a Keystore key.
  • Passwords you copy are placed on the clipboard marked as sensitive and cleared automatically after the delay you choose.
  • Android's backup and device-transfer features are switched off for the app, so the vault is not copied to Google through them.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you tap Sign in with Google in CipherON, you sign in on Google's own page in your browser. CipherON never sees your Google password. The app requests one permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: view and manage only the files and folders that CipherON itself creates in your Google Drive. CipherON cannot see any other file in your Drive.

What the app does with it: it creates a folder named CipherON in your Drive, uploads your backup files there (they are your vault in already-encrypted form, readable only with your master password), lists them so you can restore one, downloads the one you pick, and deletes old backups according to your "backups to keep" setting. It also reads the email address of the signed-in account so it can show you which account is connected.

This information goes only between your phone and Google. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising or any purpose other than providing the backup and restore feature you asked for. To disconnect, press Sign out in CipherON (this removes the sign-in from your phone) and, to revoke the access at Google as well, remove CipherON at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can delete the CipherON folder from your Drive at any time.

CipherON's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

WebDAV sync (optional)

If you enter a WebDAV server address and login, CipherON uploads the encrypted vault file to that server, which you choose and control. The server's operator can see an encrypted file but not its contents. The login password is stored encrypted on your phone.

Files you export or share

If you export to plain CSV, Excel or PDF, or share a backup, the resulting file is written where you choose and leaves the app's protection. Plain exports are not encrypted unless you select the encrypted CSV option.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet: only for the optional Google Drive backup and WebDAV sync you set up.
  • Biometric: only to offer fingerprint unlock if you turn it on.

Children

CipherON is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
